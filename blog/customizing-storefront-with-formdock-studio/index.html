Customizing Your Online Shopfront with FormDock Studio

FS
E-Commerce Engineering
Storefront Team

A compelling e-commerce storefront is vital for online sales conversion. FormDock Studio (studio.formdock.in) serves as your visual storefront editing platform, empowering developers and store owners to edit their storefront layout without complex CSS coding.

E-Commerce Backend Integration

FormDock Studio connects directly with your FormDock Core e-commerce backend features: - Product Catalog Display: Visually style product listings, prices, categories, and inventory statuses. - Promotions & Coupons: Embed discount banners and coupon redemption widgets powered by your store rules. - Orders & Checkout: Design smooth order inquiry and checkout forms that sync with your store invoices and tax settings. - Responsive Layout Control: Tailor mobile and desktop store viewports in real time.
